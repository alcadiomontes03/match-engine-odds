# Pulsematch v6

**Status (2026-10-03): paper mode, CLV gate closed. No real-money stakes.**

v6 is the name for the system as it runs from 2026-10-03: the v5 market-anchored model in `pipeline/v5`
(unchanged), run each week by the agent pipeline described in [`v5.5.md`](v5.5.md) (same agent code,
kept private outside this repository), plus the Banker and the dashboard. The model's maths did not change.

Leagues: Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Ligue 1.

## Alpha Lines v6.1, ratings fix (2026-10-12; first tagged v6.2): Bundesliga goal-rate refit, centred defence ridge, quarter-line keys

**Why the Dixon-Coles ratings were frozen.** The weekly v4 refit rebuilt its fit from about 30 Drive downloads per run
and failed on Sep 24, 25 and 28 (the Sep 28 run took 57 minutes). On 2026-10-03 it was retired at Alcadio's
choice and the ratings stayed at the 2026-09-25 fit. With blend weight 0 in every league the ratings never set a
price, so nothing was lost but freshness. The failure was the Drive-based rebuild, not the maths: the football-data
history is now in this repo (`data/fd/`), so a refit takes seconds.

**What was wrong with the fit (all four leagues).** `pipeline/src/dixon_coles.py` shrinks attack and defence toward 0.
Attack is pinned to mean 0, so that is harmless there. Defence carries the league's goal level, so shrinking it
toward 0 pulled every team's expected goals down, away goals most of all. On the Sep 25 Bundesliga fit, mean defence
was -0.14 where the data wants about -0.30: 1.21 expected away goals against 1.46 actual.

**The fix** (`pipeline/v6/refit.py`): same likelihood, window (1,100 days), decay (xi 0.002) and ridge strength (5),
but defence is shrunk toward the league-mean defence instead of toward 0. Goals-only target (the xG blend needs the
Drive xG history, which is not in the repo).

Walk-forward, monthly refits, 2023-24 to 2026-27 (4,316 matches), mean predicted minus actual total goals:

| League | Old ridge | Centred ridge | O/U 2.5 log loss old / new |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bundesliga | -0.165 | -0.052 | 0.6542 / 0.6498 |
| Premier League | -0.133 | -0.039 | 0.6810 / 0.6789 |
| La Liga | -0.124 | -0.097 | 0.6704 / 0.6703 |
| Ligue 1 | -0.106 | -0.031 | 0.6840 / 0.6821 |
| All | -0.131 | -0.056 | 0.6728 / 0.6708 |

1X2 log loss is unchanged (0.9889 to 0.9884) and BTTS improves slightly (0.6845 to 0.6829). Raw output:
`pipeline/v6/out/refit_backtest.json`. A small bias remains (goal rates have been rising); it was left alone rather
than tuned to one week.

**Applied to the dashboard: Bundesliga first** (the other three leagues followed the same day, see the next section) (`state/detail_D1`, `state/summary.leagues.D1`), refit as of 2026-10-12
with the 9 Oct 9-11 results added from the dashboard (football-data had not posted them yet). Premier League, La Liga
and Ligue 1 were refit the same way afterwards. Expected Bundesliga goals for the Oct 9-11 round,
refit as of Oct 5: 3.21 per game against 3.00 from the old fit; actual 3.89 (nine matches, so mostly variance).
The Ratings page's Defense and Net columns now sit about 0.16 lower for every Bundesliga team (the league mean
defence is -0.30, not -0.14); rankings are unchanged.

**Spread and total keys.** `side_odds.py` rounded every line to one decimal, so quarter lines were stored on the wrong
key (+0.25 as "+0.2", -0.75 as "-0.8", a 2.25 total as "2.2"). Whole and half lines keep one decimal; quarter lines
now keep two ("+0.25", "2.25"). The dashboard ignores quarter lines for pricing (it only grids half and whole
lines), so no prices changed; the stored data is now correct. The two odds docs with side prices were rewritten.

## Alpha Lines v6.1, research record (2026-10-12): every-line test, permanent record, sanity checks

Changes after an outside review (Codex). Code: `pipeline/v6/research.py`. It runs every Monday as step 6 of the
weekly grading job, which commits its output to `data/research/`.

**Permanent record.**
- **`data/research/lines.csv`:** one row per DraftKings side line (BTTS, totals, spreads, corners) of every settled
  match. Each row holds both raw DraftKings prices, its overround and Shin probability, and when the side price was
  pulled (minutes before kickoff). It also holds the moneyline used, the model's probability from the market-anchored
  grid (the same inversion as the dashboard), the Dixon-Coles and corners-model probabilities where they exist, and
  the score, total corners and outcome.
- **`data/research/picks.csv`:** every graded auto-pick, tagged with the pick rule it was logged under
  (`safest_line` before 2026-10-12, `v6.1` after).
- **Append-only by key.** Rows are never changed, and git history is the audit trail. Re-running adds nothing new.

**Pre-registered tests** (fixed before any v6.1 data existed, so the data can't choose them):
1. **Primary, goal markets:** log loss of the model's probability against DraftKings' own Shin-de-vigged price, on
   every scored BTTS, total and spread line, pooled.
   - Excluded: lines used to fit the grid (the main total, or BTTS when it was the anchor), pushes and quarter lines.
   - Reported as the mean difference (model minus DraftKings) with an interval from a bootstrap that resamples
     whole matches.
2. **Primary, corners:** the same test for the corners model against DraftKings' corners price, from week 7.
3. **Everything else is exploratory:** per market, per league, the Dixon-Coles ratings, pick returns, DraftKings'
   margins, and de-vig methods on side lines.

**Looks and decisions.** The primary tests are read only at fixed checkpoints: the week 10 check-in (from
2026-11-03), mid-season (after the January break), and the end of the season.
- At the first two checkpoints a test needs its 99% interval entirely below 0 to count as "model better".
- At the end of the season it needs the 95% interval.
- An interval entirely above 0 means DraftKings prices those lines better.
- Weekly reports are descriptive only.
- The 300-pick count is no longer the decision rule. It stays only as the CLV gate's minimum for the model's own
  paper picks.

**Sanity checks** (written to `data/research/sanity/<date>.json` and listed in the grading job's notification):
- predicted against actual goals per league, for both the ratings and the market grid;
- results missing 6 hours after kickoff, and results without match stats;
- duplicate fixtures, and result letters that don't match the score;
- prediction probabilities that don't sum to 1, and predictions carrying `gate_open: true`;
- odds overrounds outside a sane range, and weak name matches between the odds feed and the fixtures;
- auto-picks with no fixture;
- how close to kickoff the "closing" and side prices really were;
- odds API credits left.

The first run (week 6 backfill) flagged two things:
- 32 predictions carry `gate_open: true`. This is the league's old blend-validation flag copied from
  `state/summary`; blend weight is 0, so no price is affected.
- The closing and side prices were pulled a median 110 minutes before kickoff, and only 1 of 16 within 65 minutes.

**De-vig comparison** (`research.py devig`, report in `data/research/reports/devig-2026-10-12.md`). On
football-data closing prices, 2017-18 to 2026-27:
- Proportional, additive, power and Shin are within 0.0003 log loss of each other.
- Pinnacle 1X2 (12,017 matches): Shin 0.96844, proportional +0.00007.
- O/U 2.5: power is best by 0.00007-0.00009 and proportional worst by 0.00018-0.00028.
- **The de-vig choice does not change conclusions on main markets. Shin stays.** The same comparison runs on
  DraftKings' side lines every week as data builds up.

## Alpha Lines v6.1, build 51 (2026-10-12): DraftKings corners prices

From week 7 (Oct 13) the odds bot asks DraftKings for `alternate_totals_corners` (total match corners, over/under) at each match's first late pull, alongside BTTS, totals and spreads. It is in both the full and the core credit tier, so it stays on above 130 credits. It is billed one credit per match when DraftKings returns it, and dropped for the week in a league after three empty replies, like the other side markets. `side_odds.py` writes it to `odds/<id>.side.corners` (`{"9.5": {over, under}}`). The dashboard's corners card and the Ranked Bets corners filter show DraftKings' price and edge wherever its line matches a line the corners model prices, and the corners best pick follows the same rule as the other side markets. Corners auto-picks were already graded from match stats.

## Alpha Lines v6.1 addendum: La Liga goal-rate gap: what it is and the fix (2026-10-12, later)
Cause: not the ridge. La Liga's goal rate has been rising (league average 2.51 in 2021-22, 2.69 in 2025-26, 3.04 so far in 2026-27), and a 1,100-day window with xi 0.002 averages over the older low-scoring seasons, so every fit lags the current level. The leftover bias is the same size for home and away goals (-0.052 and -0.045). Premier League and Ligue 1 lag less because their goal rates moved less.

Tried and rejected: faster decay (xi 0.004 or 0.006) or a 730-day window. Total-goals bias does shrink (La Liga -0.097 to -0.063 at xi 0.006) but 1X2 log loss gets worse in every league (0.9743 to 0.9855 in La Liga, 0.9851 to 0.9961 in the Premier League), because the team ratings become noisier. Raw output: `pipeline/v6/out/refit_decay_variants.json`.

Fix: a separate goal-level factor, `level_factor()` in `pipeline/v6/refit.py` (also printed by `refit.py fit` as `goal_level_factor`). It is the time-weighted ratio of actual to predicted total goals over the last 400 days (half-life 90 days), applied to both expected goals, and leaves the ratings alone. Walk-forward 2023-27 total-goals bias, without and with the factor: La Liga -0.097 to -0.049, Premier League -0.039 to -0.013, Bundesliga -0.052 to -0.026, Ligue 1 -0.031 to -0.017.

Factors as of 2026-10-12: Premier League 1.000, La Liga 1.045, Bundesliga 1.088, Ligue 1 0.998. The Bundesliga value reflects a very hot start (3.8 goals per game); treat it as the least reliable.

Status: computed and tested only. It is not applied anywhere: the dashboard's price path ignores ratings (blend weight 0) and its fixtures job would need a code change to multiply by the factor. Decide in week 7 whether to wire it into the display and the comparison line.

## v6.1 (2026-10-12): side markets, best picks, corners

Changes from a review of the first post-break matchweek (Oct 9-12). The v5 model maths did not change.

**Odds capture**
- DraftKings never returned spreads or totals in the bulk `/odds` call for these four leagues (every pull since
  2026-09-25 came back moneyline only) but each market was billed, so the bulk call is now `h2h` only (1 credit).
- BTTS, totals and spreads (plus DraftKings' alternate lines while more than 250 credits remain) are pulled per
  match from the event endpoint at its first late pull. That endpoint bills only the markets it returns; a market
  DraftKings returns nothing for three matches in a row in a league is skipped there for the rest of the week.
- GitHub ran the "every 15 min" schedule only every 3-7 hours (30 runs between Oct 4 and Oct 12), so the 3-hour late
  window missed most Sunday matches. The first late pull now happens within 8 hours of kickoff, and the cron runs
  off the quarter-hours. `kind = late` therefore means "within 8 hours of kickoff".
- `fetch_fd.py` keeps football-data's corner counts (`HC`, `AC`) and refetches any older trim that lacks a column.

**Dashboard (Alpha Lines)**
- Totals, spreads and BTTS are priced from DraftKings' own de-vigged moneyline plus its main total line (or its BTTS
  price when no total is posted), the same inversion as `pipeline/v5/market.py`. The Dixon-Coles numbers (refit 2026-10-12 for all four leagues; see the Alpha Lines v6.1 sections above) are
  kept for comparison. With no DraftKings moneyline the ratings numbers are used as before.
- DraftKings side-market prices (`odds/<id>.side`, written by the odds sync from `pipeline/v6/side_odds.py`) show in the
  cells, so edges on BTTS, totals and spreads are measured against real prices.
- The robot's best pick per market is the biggest positive edge against a DraftKings price; with none it is the main
  line (the line whose likelier side is closest to even money). The old rule picked the most likely line, which is
  always the safest, lowest-paying one (+1.5 spreads, over 1.5 goals).
- Favourite spreads above +0.5 are flagged as not offered on DraftKings (adjustable), and every spread/total/shots/
  corners bet in Ranked Bets has a line adjuster.
- Shots, team shots on target and corners are graded from match stats; postponed fixtures void their picks; the
  prediction log shows a calibration table.

**Corners model** (`pipeline/v6/corners.py`, display and paper tracking only)
- Time-weighted Poisson team rates (half-life 240 days, ridge 80) from three seasons of football-data corners;
  each side priced as a negative binomial with the dispersion measured on match totals.
- Walk-forward on 2025-26 (1,372 matches): log loss 3.715 summed over lines 7.5-12.5 versus 3.751 for a flat
  league average, so a small but consistent improvement; still slightly under-predicts the higher lines. There
  are no DraftKings corner prices, so no edge is measured. Results in `pipeline/v6/out/corners_backtest.json`.

**Scheduled jobs**: the odds sync now runs on match days (Fri-Mon, 11:17, 15:17, 18:17 UTC) and also writes the side
prices; the Monday fixtures job writes `corners/<id>`; the Monday grading job stores match stats and marks
postponed fixtures.

## What changed in v6 (operations, not maths)

- The weekly Dixon-Coles refit (v4) was retired. Its ratings stay at the 2026-09-25 fit and only feed the
  dashboard's per-match detail; their blend weight is 0 in every league, so they never set a price.
- The scheduled jobs read odds and model code as exact files from this repository instead of re-typing
  downloads, and the Monday jobs wait for the weekly odds pull before they run.
- Closing prices for CLV come from the bot's pulls within 3 hours of kickoff (`kind` = late in
  `data/odds/snapshots.csv`); a weekly pull days before kickoff is never used as a close.

## Known risk: odds credits

A full matchweek in all four leagues can use roughly 100-140 of the free tier's 500 monthly credits
(see the README), so late in a busy month the bot's reserves can stop late pulls and leave picks without
a closing price. Options if that happens: a paid Odds API plan, turning BTTS off (`ODDS_BTTS`), or fewer
markets on late pulls.

## The gate

Unchanged from v5.5: no stakes until 300 or more graded paper picks show a positive mean CLV with t of at
least 2. v5 equals the market so far (every backtest season shrank its corrections to zero), so it makes
no moneyline bets; the paper picks come from DraftKings side markets priced against its own main lines.
